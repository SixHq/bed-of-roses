# Bed of Roses — Theme Variants Research

A short field guide to 10 directions we could ship alongside **Cartoon Brutalist**, **Warm & Earthy**, and the existing **Bloom** (aubergine + serif). Each direction is opinionated about palette, type, motion, and the *kind of person* it appeals to — so we never ship two themes that occupy the same emotional slot.

The first entry is **Neo Brutalist**, which I've already built as a working theme.

---

## 0. Neo Brutalist  *(built)*
**Mood:** raw web, file-system, structural honesty.
- **Palette:** paper white, ink black, single hot accent (electric red `#FF3B00`).
- **Type:** Helvetica Now / Inter Display 700 + JetBrains Mono.
- **Hallmarks:** 1px hairlines, hard 90° corners, monospace metadata, ASCII dividers (`/// ─── ////`), ungridded blocks, structural labels (`[01]`, `↳`, `&`), reduced color, all-caps subheads.
- **Why it works:** opposite of the cartoon version. Reads serious, technical, "proper software." Appeals to power users.

## 1. Y2K / Frutiger Aero
**Mood:** 2003 corporate optimism — translucent gel, blue glass, water droplets.
- **Palette:** electric blue `#0066FF`, silver `#C8D6E5`, lime accent `#A8E063`, white.
- **Type:** Frutiger / Inter + Eurostile-ish display.
- **Hallmarks:** glassmorphic gel buttons with specular highlights, soft drop shadows, pixel-precise icon glyphs, blue depth gradients, a bouncing "loading" ellipsis somewhere.
- **Pairs with users who:** love mid-2000s nostalgia, Sony VAIOs, the original Aero theme.

## 2. Risograph Print Shop
**Mood:** zine, gallery poster, slightly off-register.
- **Palette:** two-or-three spot inks — fluoro pink `#FF48B0`, federal blue `#3145C2`, sun yellow `#FFE15B` — printed on warm cream `#F4ECD8`.
- **Type:** ABC Diatype-ish grotesque + a quirky display (Fraunces / Bagoss). Italic captions.
- **Hallmarks:** halftone fills, intentional 1–2px misregistration on overlapping shapes, dotted gradients, paper grain, no pure black (always a deep ink).
- **Pairs with:** designers, zine makers, people who buy It's Nice That.

## 3. Pixel CRT / Retro Terminal
**Mood:** 1995 BBS, Severance terminal, comfort of phosphor.
- **Palette:** phosphor green `#33FF66` (or amber `#FFB000`) on `#0B0B0B`, magenta error.
- **Type:** IBM Plex Mono / VT323 / Departure Mono everywhere.
- **Hallmarks:** scanlines, blink cursor, 8×8 pixel icons, ASCII frames, no antialiasing on display sizes, optional CRT curvature filter.
- **Pairs with:** terminal lovers, dev users, people who use Tmux for fun.

## 4. Liquid Glass (Apple Vision-era)
**Mood:** Apple WWDC25, Vision Pro, weightless depth.
- **Palette:** near-white surfaces over dynamic chromatic backgrounds; specular tints from the wallpaper bleed through.
- **Type:** SF Pro Display / Inter Display in restrained weights.
- **Hallmarks:** thick frosted glass with parallax refraction, edge-light specular hairlines, generous corner radii, cards float and tilt with cursor, deep saturated background image required for the glass to sing.
- **Pairs with:** Apple-fluent users; needs photo or color-rich background to read right.

## 5. Memphis / 80s Postmodern
**Mood:** Ettore Sottsass, MTV bumper, terrazzo lobby.
- **Palette:** mint `#5FE3A1`, hot pink `#FF4FA3`, lemon `#FFE15B`, electric blue `#3DB7FF`, on warm cream.
- **Type:** Migra / Editorial New display + Beatrice grotesque.
- **Hallmarks:** squiggles, dotted patterns, terrazzo confetti, tilted shapes, asymmetric cards with one rounded + one squared corner, 3D primitives floating in the corner.
- **Pairs with:** playful, ostentatious; cousin to Cartoon Brutalist but more pattern-driven.

## 6. Solarpunk / Greenhouse
**Mood:** Studio Ghibli, hopeful future, ferns through the window.
- **Palette:** moss `#3F5D3A`, terracotta `#C66B4A`, beeswax `#F2D58A`, dawn pink `#F4B5A0`, parchment.
- **Type:** Söhne / Migra display with custom serif fern flourishes; body in IBM Plex Sans.
- **Hallmarks:** botanical line illustrations as section dividers, golden-hour gradients, paper grain, hand-rendered iconography, copy that addresses the user as a gardener of their own day.
- **Pairs with:** Warm & Earthy users who want a softer, more illustrative cousin.

## 7. Cyberpunk Neon Noir
**Mood:** Blade Runner 2049, Tokyo at night, GitHub dark + neon.
- **Palette:** charcoal `#0A0612` base, cyan `#00E5FF`, magenta `#FF2D9C`, occasional electric lime.
- **Type:** Space Grotesk + Söhne Mono; double-byte spacer characters.
- **Hallmarks:** chromatic aberration on headlines, scanline shimmer on hover, glitch-on-error states, hex-grid backgrounds, neon outline on focused elements.
- **Pairs with:** night owls, devs, the Cartoon Brutalist's evil twin.

## 8. Editorial / Magazine
**Mood:** *The New Yorker*, Apartamento, museum gift shop.
- **Palette:** ivory `#F5F1E8`, oxblood `#7B1E2D`, gold leaf `#C9A96E`, ink.
- **Type:** Sentinel or Lyon Display headlines + Untitled Sans body. Drop caps.
- **Hallmarks:** generous margins, captioned everything, hairline rules between sections, pull-quote treatment, Roman numerals as section markers, sparse imagery treated reverently.
- **Pairs with:** Bloom users who want even more editorial restraint.

## 9. Claymorphism / Pillow
**Mood:** soft 3D toys, plush UI, Notion-style stickers.
- **Palette:** muted pastels — sage, blush, butter, sky, lilac — on cream.
- **Type:** Söhne Breit / Bricolage rounded weights + Söhne body.
- **Hallmarks:** every surface is a soft 3D extrusion (dual shadows: top-light + bottom-shadow), generously rounded corners, plump iconography, gentle squish on press.
- **Pairs with:** people who like Notion, Things 3, calm tactile interfaces.

## 10. Newsprint Manifesto
**Mood:** zine, Adbusters cover, oversized headline.
- **Palette:** newsprint cream `#EDE5D0`, ink black, single hot red `#D9281C`. Optionally one yellow highlight.
- **Type:** big bold serif (Tiempos Headline) + condensed grotesque body. Italic kicker.
- **Hallmarks:** column rules, pull-quotes, halftone duotone photos, pull-tabs at corners, "FROM THE EDITOR" voice, oversized H1 that takes up half the page.
- **Pairs with:** punks, organizers, people who like one-page manifestos.

## How to pick (a quick map)

| If the user wants… | Steer them to |
|---|---|
| Serious power-user, ships software | **Neo Brutalist** |
| Quiet, literary, calm | **Bloom** or **Editorial** |
| Cozy, lived-in, journal | **Warm & Earthy** or **Solarpunk** |
| Loud, fun, sticker energy | **Cartoon Brutalist** or **Memphis** |
| Nostalgic / specific decade | **Y2K**, **Pixel CRT**, **Newsprint** |
| Deeply visual / image-heavy | **Liquid Glass**, **Risograph** |
| Dark-mode lover | **Cyberpunk Neon Noir** |
| Tactile, soft, cozy-tech | **Claymorphism** |

## Implementation notes
- Each theme is a tokens file + a kit JSX following the same component contract (Hero, Logo, HUD, Colors, Type, Buttons, Fields, Chips, Tiles, Bubbles, Chat bar, Providers, Avatar, Marks).
- The shared design-canvas wrapper means new themes drop in without rebuilding the showcase scaffold.
- Recommend shipping themes in waves of 3 so QA stays sane: Neo Brutalist + Y2K + Risograph as Wave 2; Pixel CRT + Liquid Glass + Memphis as Wave 3; etc.
