**Bed of Roses (BOR) is an AI companion for macOS — not a chat window.** A small, draggable presence floats above your real desktop, always one keystroke away. You tell it what you want, and it does the work in front of you.

<img src="/assets/bor_sota_code_writing_demo.gif" alt="Bed of Roses working live on the desktop: streaming its reasoning, writing files into editor cards, and running commands while the result takes shape" />

## What a conversation can do

Every request can end in something real, not just text:

- **An app** — "make me a calorie tracker" opens a native Mac window, built for you, saved forever. [Building apps](/features/building-apps)
- **Action in your services** — with Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion, GitHub and ~100 more connected, "reply to Sarah" uses the real Gmail, not a browser. [Connectors](/features/connectors)
- **Web work** — BOR drives a real Chrome with its own profile: reads pages, fills forms, stays signed in, verifies results. [Ambient browser](/features/ambient-browser)
- **File work** — it reads PDF, Word, Excel, HEIC, and almost anything else; writes and edits files; every path it mentions clicks through to Finder. [Files](/features/files)
- **Mac control** — screen capture, vision-guided clicks and typing, opening apps. [Computer use](/features/computer-use)
- **Media** — YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, tweets, and video play as cards inside the presence. [Media](/features/media)
- **Scheduled work** — "every morning at 8…" runs real agent turns while you're away and notifies you. [Background tasks](/features/background-tasks)
- **A new look** — eight themes, generated wallpapers applied to your actual desktop. [Themes](/features/themes)

And you watch all of it happen: the thought bubble streams BOR's reasoning, the code it writes, the commands it runs, and the pages it visits as live cards — with a stop button that pauses any task and a play button that resumes it. [The presence](/features/presence)

## Yours, locally

- **Your model.** Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (DeepSeek, Kimi, Z.ai, Ollama presets) — or Bed of Roses credits with no key at all. Switch models from a chip in the input bar. [Providers](/providers/overview)
- **Your machine.** Everything runs locally. Keys stay in local config, generated apps are sandboxed, and every action lands in an audit log.
- **Your memory.** It remembers you across sessions — and you can run [several presences](/features/multi-presence), each a fully separate companion.
