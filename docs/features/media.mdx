When Bed of Roses finds or is asked for media, it plays it right where you are: as a card inside the thought bubble.

<img src="/assets/play_media_directly_inside_bor.png" alt="A video playing inside the presence's thought bubble as a media card" />

## What plays inline

- **YouTube** — regular videos, Shorts, and live streams (privacy-friendly embeds).
- **Vimeo**.
- **TikTok** — videos via TikTok's embed player.
- **Instagram** — posts and reels.
- **Tweets** — rendered with the official embed, matching your light/dark theme.
- **Images** — direct image URLs (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP).
- **Video files** — direct `.mp4` links in a native player.

BOR chooses what's worth showing — search results and tool output don't auto-embed every URL they touch; when BOR surfaces media, it decided you should see it.

## What to say

- "Play the new Daft Punk video."
- "Find me a good 10-minute stretching routine and play it."
- "Show me that tweet everyone's talking about."
- "Play the mp4 I just downloaded." (local video files open via the [Files](/features/files) side; web media plays inline)

## Media BOR makes

Generated images appear as image cards too, with follow-up actions (use as wallpaper, save, regenerate). Screen captures from [computer use](/features/computer-use) render the same way, so you always see what BOR is seeing.
