# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Bed of Roses to be a welcoming, friendly, and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of background or identity. As contributors and maintainers,
we pledge to make participation a positive experience and to act in ways that
keep the community open and respectful.

## Our standard

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant]** as its
code of conduct. That document defines the behavior we expect, the behavior we
will not tolerate, and the responsibilities of maintainers. By participating, you
agree to uphold it. Please read the full text here:

- https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be kind, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback
gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions — and in public spaces when an individual is
representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to
the maintainers at **conduct@sixhq.example**.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and
may take any action they deem appropriate, up to and including a temporary or
permanent ban from the project, for behavior that violates it. Enforcement follows
the guidelines described in the Contributor Covenant linked above.
