Allow computer control.

To watch your screen and click/type in real Mac apps (computer-use), Bed of Roses needs two macOS permissions. Grant them now, or skip and do it later — chat, browser-use, and files all work without them.

Control your Mac checking…

Lets me click, type, and press keys inside apps on your behalf. This needs two macOS permissions — Automation (allow Bed of Roses to control "System Events") and Accessibility. Without both you'll see "not authorized to send Apple events" (-1743) errors.

Screen Recording checking…

Lets me see your screen so I can find what to click. Without it, computer-use is operating blind.

Toggle each permission on in System Settings, then quit and reopen Bed of Roses for it to take effect, and click Re-check. These are optional — you can skip and grant them later.
