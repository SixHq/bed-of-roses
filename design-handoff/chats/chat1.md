Here are the 10 directions from `variants-research.md`:

1. **Y2K / Frutiger Aero** — translucent gel, blue glass, water droplets; Sony-VAIO-era optimism.
2. **Risograph Print Shop** — fluoro pink + federal blue spot inks on cream, halftone, intentional misregistration.
3. **Pixel CRT / Retro Terminal** — phosphor green on black, scanlines, pixel icons, VT323/Departure Mono.
4. **Liquid Glass (Apple Vision-era)** — thick frosted glass, refraction, parallax, edge-light specular hairlines.
5. **Memphis / 80s Postmodern** — squiggles, terrazzo, mint/pink/lemon/blue, asymmetric tilted shapes.
6. **Solarpunk / Greenhouse** — moss + terracotta + beeswax, botanical line illustrations, golden-hour gradients.
7. **Cyberpunk Neon Noir** — charcoal + cyan + magenta, chromatic aberration, glitch-on-error, hex-grid backgrounds.
8. **Editorial / Magazine** — ivory + oxblood + gold leaf, large serif, drop caps, generous margins, Roman numeral sections.
9. **Claymorphism / Pillow** — muted pastels, soft 3D extrusions, dual shadows, plump iconography, gentle press squish.
10. **Newsprint Manifesto** — newsprint cream + ink + hot red, big bold serif, halftone duotone photos, "FROM THE EDITOR" voice.
