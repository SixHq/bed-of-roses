You've [installed](/getting-started/installation) BOR and finished [onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding). The orb is on your desktop. Click it — or press **Cmd+Option+Space** — and try these.

## Five things to try

### 1. Ask a question

> "What's the weather for a hike near me this weekend?"

BOR searches the web, reads sources, and answers concisely in the bubble. Not everything builds an app — a good answer is a valid result.

### 2. Build an app

> "Make me a calorie tracker."

BOR researches what a good calorie tracker needs, builds a complete app, and opens it as its own Mac window. It's saved — reopen it any time from the App Library in the bubble header, or just ask. See [Building apps](/features/building-apps).

### 3. Use a connected service

> "What's in my inbox this morning?"

If you linked Gmail during onboarding, BOR reads it through the real Gmail connection — no browser theater. Haven't linked anything yet? See [Connectors](/features/connectors).

### 4. Send it to the web

> "Find the cheapest direct flight to Lisbon in September."

BOR opens its own Chrome and works the sites like a person — reading pages structurally, clicking, filling forms — and reports back with verified results. See [Ambient browser](/features/ambient-browser).

### 5. Schedule something

> "Every weekday at 9, summarize my inbox and notify me."

BOR schedules a real agent run. Results land behind the bell; hit **Continue in chat** on any notification to pick the thread up. See [Background tasks](/features/background-tasks).

## Reading the thought bubble

While BOR works, the bubble streams live cards: **reasoning** (its thinking), **editors** (files being written line by line), **terminals** (commands with live output), **browser** sessions, **MCP calls** to your connected services, images, and media. You always see what it's doing and why.

Useful controls, all in the bubble header:

- **Stop** (square) cancels the running task; the button becomes **play** — press it to resume exactly where it stopped.
- **Minimize** collapses a running task to a chip; the work continues.
- **History** reopens past conversations with every card intact.
- **App Library**, **Notifications**, and the **MCP marketplace** live here too.

Drag any file onto the presence to attach it as context, or use the **+** button. The **model chip** in the input switches models mid-conversation. See [The presence](/features/presence) for everything.
